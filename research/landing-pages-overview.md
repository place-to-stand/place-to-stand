# Book-a-Call Landing Pages Overview

## Current landing page index
- `http://localhost:3000/book-a-call`

## Variant list (old + new)

1. **Fast Start**
   - Route: `/book-a-call/fast-start`
   - Aimed for: Owners who need momentum in the next 2-4 weeks and are losing opportunities due to slow response and execution.

2. **Profit Focus**
   - Route: `/book-a-call/profit-focus`
   - Aimed for: Margin-focused owners who care about measurable ROI, time savings, and revenue impact.

3. **Done For You**
   - Route: `/book-a-call/done-for-you`
   - Aimed for: Teams that need full execution support and do not have internal bandwidth to implement automation themselves.

4. **Stress-Free Growth**
   - Route: `/book-a-call/stress-free-growth`
   - Aimed for: Owners dealing with day-to-day chaos and inconsistent handoffs who want calmer, standardized operations.

5. **AI Co-Worker**
   - Route: `/book-a-call/ai-coworker`
   - Aimed for: Businesses that need more output without adding immediate headcount.

6. **Automation Pipeline**
   - Route: `/book-a-call/automation-pipeline`
   - Aimed for: Service businesses leaking leads between inquiry and booked call due to manual handoffs.

7. **Ops Systemization**
   - Route: `/book-a-call/ops-systemization`
   - Aimed for: Teams dependent on tribal knowledge that need documented, repeatable workflows.

8. **Automation ROI**
   - Route: `/book-a-call/automation-roi`
   - Aimed for: Owners skeptical of automation spend who need clear before/after business outcomes.

9. **Never Miss a Lead**
   - Route: `/book-a-call/never-miss-a-lead`
   - Aimed for: Teams with weak follow-up discipline and lead leakage from delayed first response.

10. **AI COO**
    - Route: `/book-a-call/ai-coo`
    - Aimed for: Small teams with knowledge chaos across tools that need centralized visibility and blocker tracking.

11. **Overnight Execution**
    - Route: `/book-a-call/overnight-execution`
    - Aimed for: Owners who want queued workflows to run after hours so mornings start with completed prep work.

12. **Approval-First Automation**
    - Route: `/book-a-call/approval-first-automation`
    - Aimed for: Risk-sensitive teams that want automation speed while preserving human control on sensitive actions.
