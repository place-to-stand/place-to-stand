We have spent the last year shipping bespoke software for small and mid-sized
businesses and watching what actually moves the needle once it lands in
production. This blog is where we will write that work up — short pieces about
process, longer ones about architecture, and the occasional opinionated take on
where the AI tooling space is going.

## What you can expect here

- **Notes from the four-day production cycle.** What worked, what blew up, and
  what we changed.
- **Operator-level posts on AI.** How we wire LLMs into real businesses
  without the demoware energy.
- **Engineering deep-dives.** When a build was interesting enough to warrant
  one — not for its own sake.

## What you will not find

We are not chasing a publishing cadence. If we do not have something useful to
say, we will not say it. Expect bursts when we ship something we are proud of.

Subscribe via RSS if you want it the moment it goes up, or just check back when
you remember.
